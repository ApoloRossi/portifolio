// about

Fast screens, smooth scrolling, messages that arrive once.

I'm an Android engineer who has spent nine years shipping apps for very large audiences, from a mobile banking app with 50M+ active users to a telecom messaging platform with 100M+ users around the world.

I work mostly in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, and I care about the things users notice without knowing why: loading times, jank on the main thread, and reliability. I have also designed a base architecture from scratch and led a team of seven Android developers.

Performance

Profiling with Perfetto and Android Profiler to remove main-thread jank and shorten loading times in critical flows.

Architecture

MVVM, Clean Architecture, modularization and SOLID, so codebases stay easy to change as products and teams grow.

Reliability

Idempotent message delivery, JUnit, MockK and Espresso tests, and crash monitoring with Firebase Crashlytics.
